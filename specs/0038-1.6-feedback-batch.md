- **Status:** Draft
- **Owner agent(s):** frontend-engineer + rust-core-engineer + llm-pipeline-engineer + audio-engineer (multi-workstream)
- **Roadmap phase:** 1.6 feedback batch
- **Graduated out of this batch (own specs, cross-linked — not re-specified here):** two items
  from this feedback round were large enough to graduate to their own numbers, mirroring how
  0019 spun WS4/5/7 out to 0020–0022:
  - **`specs/0039-diarization-accuracy-and-correction.md`** — diarization accuracy & manual

  - **`specs/0040-notes-as-first-class-type.md`** — notes as a first-class object type. Any

## Context / Problem

A sustained run of real-meeting use on the **1.5 build** produced the owner's fifth dogfood
feedback batch (1.1 ← `specs/0019`, 1.2 ← `specs/0024`, 1.3 ← `specs/0029`, plus the 1.4/1.5
feature waves that shipped action items, FTS5 search, the aggregation engine, Google Calendar,
and pre-call prep). Unlike the earlier batches, most items here are **feature polish on things
that now exist and get used daily** — the feedback is "this is good, now make it usable at my
volume" — rather than data-loss/lifecycle bugs. Every item below maps onto an **existing spec**
and reuses its data model; this spec's job is to graft the asks onto those foundations without
inventing parallel structures.

Every `file:line` anchor was verified against the tree at spec-authoring time (branch
`fix/0037-recording-save-target`). Where a workstream adds a *new* surface rather than fixing a
bug, the grounding is labelled **Current state (confirmed)** — what exists to build on — instead
of a root cause.

Headline framing per workstream owner:

1. **Action items are the daily driver now** (0034 shipped), and the ask is volume ergonomics:
   due dates, a compact list I can hand-order, an "assigned to me" default, a way to add one
   from Home, and — emphatically — *"I'm not going to keep up with everyone else's action
   items; let me purge the ones owned by other people."*
2. **Ask-AI works but is amnesiac** (0035): every question replaces the last, and there's no way
   to pin a recurring question ("what did we decide today?") and re-run it against fresh data.
3. **Calendar fidelity is the perennial pain** (0019/0024/0029/0032): with Google connected, a
   distribution list still shows as one fake attendee (0027 never actually closed for
   Google-sourced events), and attendee rows are faceless (no photos).
4. **Today-only is too short a horizon** (0036): pre-call prep is most valuable *before*
   tomorrow's or next week's meeting, but the Today view can't look past today.
5. **People need prioritization** (0012/0016): at real volume the attendee/speaker pick-lists
   are alphabetical noise — *"who are my top 30?"* — and there's no "recent meetings with X"
   roll-up.
6. **Live-transcript UX has rough edges** (0011/0025/0017; refines 0029 WS4.1/4.2): auto-scroll
   modes, spectrometer responsiveness + inconsistent bar widths, and identify-a-speaker not
   adding them to participants.
7. **Chrome is still half-meetily** (0031/0006): Permissions clutters the main nav, the About
   modal still carries a live analytics/telemetry toggle (leftover meetily PostHog), About isn't
   in Settings, and an in-recording Language modal pops but can't leave Auto-detect.
8. **Meeting rows are anonymous** (0018): All Meetings rows show no attendees, and a 1:1 on the
   agenda shows 2 people because "me" isn't excluded.

## Goals

- Action items usable at real volume: a due date, a compact custom-orderable list, an
  "assigned to me" quick view, a Home add shortcut, and a one-action bulk-dismiss of items
  owned by other people.
- Ask-AI remembers: prior Q&A is browsable, and starred questions re-run on demand against
  fresh data. Ask-AI is reachable from Home.
- Calendar attendees are as faithful as Google's API allows: DL members expanded where exposed,
  with a documented limitation + manual-add fallback where they aren't; attendee photos shown
  where a sanctioned scope provides them.
- The agenda navigates to tomorrow and out to a week; pre-call prep can be read ahead of time.
- People self-prioritize: starred + call-frequency ranking floats top collaborators to the top
  of every pick-list; a person roll-up answers "recent meetings / 1:1s with X" via the 0035
  engine.
- The live transcript has explicit follow/released scroll modes with a jump-to-latest return, a
  responsive spectrometer with uniform bars, and identifying a speaker adds that person to the
  meeting participants.
- The app stops phoning home (analytics ripped out), Permissions leaves the main nav for
  Settings, About lives in Settings, and Language is one global Settings value.
- Meeting rows show who was there; a 1:1 shows the *other* person, not "me + them".

## Non-goals

- **Diarization accuracy & correction** — graduated to `specs/0039`. Only WS6.3 (identify →
  add participant) stays here.
- **Notes as a first-class type** — graduated to `specs/0040`.
- **Topic tags v1** (organize meetings with topic tags; topic roll-up + topic Ask-AI) — this
  is a **separate, already-queued roadmap item on its own track** (`specs/0013` Wave 3 model:
  `topics` + `meeting_topics`). The owner raised it in this batch, but it is *not* part of 1.6;
  it gets its own spec. WS5.2's *person* roll-up is a deliberately parallel, cheaper consumer of
  the same 0035 engine that the topic roll-up will use — building it here de-risks that later
  spec without pre-empting it.
- **Due-date reminders / notifications / calendar math** — WS1 adds a stored, sortable date; it
  does not schedule anything (same posture 0034 took with `due_hint`).
- **Ask-AI chat threads / follow-up turns** — WS2 persists discrete Q&A + saved questions, not
  conversational threads (0035 non-goal, unchanged).
- **A Google Directory/Admin integration for deterministic DL expansion** — WS3 investigates
  what the *already-granted* Calendar scope exposes and, if that's insufficient, documents the
  limitation and recommends an ADR-0010 amendment rather than silently widening scope.
- **Rewriting the capture/mix/VAD/STT core** (kept per `/CLAUDE.md`). WS6.2 tunes the
  spectrometer render + emit gain, not the pipeline.
- **Per-transcript or per-summary language** — explicitly out of scope for WS7.d; Language
  becomes a single global value and the per-recording modal is removed. (Summary output
  language, a separate existing setting, is untouched.)

## Approach

Each workstream is a graft onto an existing spec's data model and command surface. The two
schema-touching workstreams add **columns to existing tables** (`action_items`, `people`) or a
**small new persistence pair** (`ask_ai_history` + `saved_questions`) rather than new subsystems;
WS3/WS4/WS6/WS7/WS8 are read-path, UI, or deletion changes with no or minimal migration. WS5.2
and (later) topic roll-ups are new `AggregationPrompt`s over the 0035 engine — zero new
gather/chunk code, exactly as 0035/0036 established. WS7's analytics removal is a pure deletion
(the module is live meetily PostHog telemetry, which violates the `/CLAUDE.md` privacy invariant
and must go regardless of the other asks). No new outbound destination is introduced anywhere
except WS3's *investigation* of a Google People/Directory scope, which — if pursued — is an
ADR-0010 amendment, not a decision this spec makes.

## WS1 — Action items polish (P1)

> Owner: rust-core-engineer (schema, repo, commands) + frontend-engineer (row + hub + Home).
> **Extends `specs/0034` (Done).** Reuse the `action_items` table and the shared row/hub
> components; do **not** create a parallel task store.

### WS1.a — Due-date field

**Current state (confirmed):** 0034 deliberately stored only a verbatim `due_hint`
(`migrations/20260707000000_add_action_items.sql`, "no date parsing in v1"); there is no sortable
date. `ActionItem` (`database/repositories/action_item.rs:39`+, `SELECT_ITEM` at `:92-98`) and the
create/update commands (`src/action_items/commands.rs:91,142`, `due_hint` params at `:97,150`)
thread the hint through as an opaque string.

**Approach:** Add a nullable `due_date TEXT` (ISO-8601 date, no time) **alongside** `due_hint`
(keep the hint as the extracted provenance; `due_date` is the user/parser-set structured value).
A UI date control on the row sets `due_date`; the extractor may opportunistically fill it when the
hint is unambiguously a date, but that stays best-effort — no reminders, no notifications (0034
posture). Sorting/filtering by due date becomes possible in the hub.

**Files:** new forward-only migration `frontend/src-tauri/migrations/<next>_add_action_item_due_date_and_order.sql`
(`ALTER TABLE action_items ADD COLUMN due_date TEXT; ADD COLUMN sort_order INTEGER;` — combined
with WS1.b), `database/repositories/action_item.rs` (extend `SELECT_ITEM` :92, the `ActionItem`
struct :39, `create` :187, `update_content` :226, `replace_extracted` :337 projections),
`src/action_items/commands.rs` (`api_create_action_item` :91 + `api_update_action_item` :142 gain
`due_date`), the shared row `frontend/src/components/ActionItems/ActionItemRow.tsx` (a compact date
control), `frontend/src/lib/action-items.ts`, `frontend/src/types/` (`ActionItem` type).

### WS1.b — Compact layout + custom manual ordering / custom sort

**Current state (confirmed):** the hub (`frontend/src/app/tasks/page.tsx`) sorts server-side by
meeting recency and groups client-side by source meeting (`groupActionItems`); the repo `list`
hard-codes `ORDER BY (ai.meeting_id IS NULL) ASC, m.created_at DESC, ai.created_at ASC, ai.id ASC`
(`action_item.rs:172`). There is no user-controllable order and no manual drag order. Source-meeting
is currently the *grouping/sort key*.

**Approach:** Add a nullable `sort_order INTEGER` (same migration as WS1.a). Introduce an explicit
sort control in the hub — **Manual** (by `sort_order`, NULLs last then `created_at`), **Due date**,
**Meeting date** (today's default). In Manual mode, source-meeting becomes a **chip on the row**
(reuse the existing meeting deep-link chip) rather than the sort key, and rows are drag-reorderable
(persist via a new `api_reorder_action_items(ids_in_order)` that writes dense `sort_order` values in
one transaction). Compact the row (single-line default, expand on focus). The per-meeting section
(`ActionItemsSection.tsx`) keeps its simple in-meeting order.

**Files:** migration (shared with WS1.a), `database/repositories/action_item.rs` (`list` ordering
+ a `reorder` transaction), new `api_reorder_action_items` in `src/action_items/commands.rs`,
`frontend/src/app/tasks/page.tsx` (sort control, drag handles, meeting-as-chip layout),
`frontend/src/components/ActionItems/ActionItemRow.tsx`, `frontend/src/hooks/useActionItemMutations`
(reorder mutation).

### WS1.c — "Assigned to me" quick view

**Current state (confirmed — partially exists):** `api_list_action_items` already accepts
`mine_only` (`commands.rs:53`, doc at `:49-52`; repo `list` applies `AND ai.assignee_is_self = 1`
at `action_item.rs:169`), and the hub has an "anyone / me / person" `PersonFilter`
(`tasks/page.tsx:82`). What's missing is *promotion*: "mine" isn't a first-class default view.

**Approach:** Elevate "Assigned to me" to a top-level segmented view in the hub (Mine / Everyone /
By person), defaulting to **Mine + Open** (the owner's stated primary workflow), backed by the
existing `mine_only` + `status` filters — mostly a UI change, no new command. "Me" resolves through
owner identity (`assignee_is_self`, `specs/0018`), which the owner is *not* a `people` row for
(0034/0018 exclude-self design), so this must stay the `assignee_is_self` flag, never a `people` FK.

**Files:** `frontend/src/app/tasks/page.tsx` (default view + segmented control),
`frontend/src/components/ActionItems/` (filter bar). No backend change beyond WS1.e.

### WS1.d — Home-screen "add an action item" shortcut

**Current state (confirmed):** action items are created only from the hub's `AddActionItemRow`
(`tasks/page.tsx:248`) or a meeting section; Home (the 0036 Today view, `frontend/src/app/page.tsx`)
has no add affordance. `api_create_action_item` already supports a standalone item
(`meeting_id` nullable, `commands.rs:91`).

**Approach:** Add a lightweight "Add to-do" affordance on the Today view (a small inline
`AddActionItemRow` or a ⌘K action) that creates a standalone manual item via the existing command
and toasts a link to the hub. Reuse `AddActionItemRow` + `useActionItemMutations`; no new backend.

**Files:** `frontend/src/app/page.tsx`, `frontend/src/components/ActionItems/AddActionItemRow.tsx`
(reused), optionally `frontend/src/components/CommandPalette/index.tsx` (a "New to-do" action next
to the Ask-AI action at `:382-387`).

### WS1.e — Purge / bulk-dismiss items owned by other people

**Current state (confirmed):** the only status mutation is per-item `api_set_action_item_status`
(`commands.rs`, repo `set_status` at `action_item.rs:270`). There is no bulk operation and no
"not me" filter. The owner: *"I'm not going to keep up with everyone else's action items."*

**Approach:** Add `api_bulk_set_action_item_status(filter, status)` where `filter` selects by
`assignee_person_id` (a specific person), `not_self` (everyone but me), or an explicit id list —
writing `status='dismissed'` (protecting the rows from re-extraction, per 0034's protected/pristine
rule) in one transaction, returning the affected count for an Undo toast. Surface it in the hub as
"Dismiss all not assigned to me" and a per-person "Dismiss all from {person}". Dismiss (not delete)
so the 0034 re-extraction diff won't resurrect them (`action_item.rs:337 replace_extracted` treats
`status != 'open'` as protected).

**Files:** new `api_bulk_set_action_item_status` in `src/action_items/commands.rs`, a
`bulk_set_status` transaction in `database/repositories/action_item.rs` (mirror `set_status` :270),
`frontend/src/app/tasks/page.tsx` (bulk action + Undo), `frontend/src/hooks/useActionItemMutations`.

## WS2 — Ask-AI history & saved questions (P2)

> Owner: rust-core-engineer (history/saved-question persistence + commands) + frontend-engineer
> (Ask page history/saved UI + Home entry). **Extends `specs/0035` (Done).** The 0035 engine and
> `execute_ask_ai` are reused verbatim; this adds a thin persistence layer + re-run.

### WS2.a — Persist Q&A history

**Current state (confirmed):** Ask-AI is entirely ephemeral by design — "single question → single
answer, ephemeral (nothing persisted)" (`specs/0035`). `api_ask_ai_run` (`aggregation/commands.rs:202`)
spawns `execute_ask_ai` (`:109`) and streams `ask-ai-complete` (`answerMarkdown` + `sources`); the
`/ask` page (`frontend/src/app/ask/page.tsx:147`) holds the answer only in `useState`. Each new
question overwrites the last on screen.

**Approach:** Persist each completed run to a new `ask_ai_history` table (question, scope JSON,
answer markdown, sources JSON, provider/model, timestamp) at the point `ask-ai-complete` is emitted
in `execute_ask_ai`'s success arm — fire-and-forget, never blocking or failing the run (the 0034
background-write discipline). The `/ask` page gains a collapsible history list (most recent first)
that reloads a prior Q&A read-only. No embeddings, no re-answer on load (that's WS2.b's job).

**Files:** new migration `frontend/src-tauri/migrations/<next>_add_ask_ai_history.sql`; new
`database/repositories/ask_ai_history.rs` (insert/list/get/delete); persistence call in
`execute_ask_ai` (`aggregation/commands.rs:109`, after the answer is built); new
`api_list_ask_ai_history` / `api_delete_ask_ai_history` commands registered in `lib.rs`;
`frontend/src/app/ask/page.tsx` (history panel), `frontend/src/lib/askAi.ts` (thin wrappers).

### WS2.b — Starred / saved questions that re-run against fresh data

**Current state (confirmed):** no concept of a reusable question; the scope + question live only in
the page's `useState` (`ask/page.tsx:80-84`, `scope` memo at `:133`).

**Approach:** A second small table `saved_questions` (label, question text, scope JSON, timestamps,
`last_run_at`). "Star this question" from the Ask page persists the current question + scope; a
"Saved questions" list lets the user **re-run** one — which simply re-invokes the existing
`api_ask_ai_run` with the stored question + scope, so it re-gathers and re-answers against whatever
data now exists ("What were the key decisions today?" evaluates today, every time). Saved questions
are the seam a future scheduled/digest feature would build on (noted, not built).

**Files:** the same new migration (both tables together); `database/repositories/saved_question.rs`;
`api_create_saved_question` / `api_list_saved_questions` / `api_delete_saved_question` in
`aggregation/commands.rs` (or a sibling), registered in `lib.rs`; `frontend/src/app/ask/page.tsx`
(star button + saved list, "Run" re-invokes `api_ask_ai_run`).

### WS2.c — Ask-AI entry point on Home

**Current state (confirmed):** Ask-AI is reachable via the ⌘K action (`CommandPalette/index.tsx:382-387`,
pushes `/ask?q=…`) and the sidebar "Ask AI" nav item (`Sidebar/index.tsx:54`). The Today view Home
(`app/page.tsx`) has no direct entry.

**Approach:** Add a compact "Ask about your meetings…" entry on the Today view header that routes to
`/ask` (optionally pre-filling a saved question). Pure UI.

**Files:** `frontend/src/app/page.tsx`.

## WS3 — Calendar fidelity: DL expansion + attendee photos (P3)

> Owner: rust-core-engineer (Google provider) + frontend-engineer (attendee UI).
> **Extends `specs/0032` (Google Calendar) and `specs/0027` (DL expansion, still open for
> Google-sourced events).** Model per the **ADR-0010 amendment**: widen the Google grant with two
> read-only scopes used **optimistically / best-effort** — attempt richer data when the account and
> org allow it, degrade silently otherwise. **A granted scope is NOT access:** org policy returns
> `403 PERMISSION_DENIED` even when the scope was consented, so capability is detected at the
> **runtime** layer, per-item, with a graceful fallback to the labeled floor. An account without
> access behaves exactly as calendar-only today, with **zero extra egress**. DL flattening and
> attendee photos are **sibling workstreams** over the same foundation (scopes + probe); this
> section covers DL flattening — photos land beside it (WS3.b) reusing the same capability plumbing.

### Shared foundation (scopes + capability probe)

- **Scopes** (`calendar/google/oauth.rs:60,63` — `SCOPES` now `[&str; 4]`): the calendar pair plus
  the amendment's two best-effort scopes — `cloud-identity.groups.readonly` (DL flatten) and
  `directory.readonly` (attendee photos, WS3.b). Requested at consent; **used only when runtime
  access is confirmed**. Anything broader still needs a fresh ADR-0010 amendment.
- **Capability storage** (migration `20260709000300_add_google_capabilities.sql`): nullable columns
  on `google_calendar_account` — `can_expand_groups`, `can_fetch_photos`, `capabilities_probed_at`
  (`NULL` = not yet probed → behave as calendar-only). Repo getters/setters `get_capabilities` /
  `set_capabilities`.
- **Connect-time probe** (`calendar/google/capabilities.rs`, spawned from `commands.rs::finish_connect`;
  a stale-gated `probe_if_stale` also runs at background-sync startup for pre-existing accounts):
  after tokens are live, run ONE lightweight probe and persist flags — `can_expand_groups` via a
  `groups:lookup` (on a real DL attendee if the cache has one, else a benign lookup on the owner
  address: `403` → `false`, `2xx`/`404` → `true`, transient → leave unprobed); `can_fetch_photos`
  via a single `people:listDirectoryPeople?pageSize=1` (`200` → `true`, `403` → `false`). Non-blocking
  and non-fatal; re-probe only when stale (>7 days), never every sync.
- **Capabilities command**: `api_google_capabilities() -> { canExpandGroups, canFetchPhotos, probedAt }`
  (registered in `lib.rs`) for the Settings surface. Instant/local, no network.

### WS3.a — Distribution list still renders as one fake attendee (Google)

**Current state (confirmed):** 0027 was expected to close when Google Calendar landed (0032), but it
did **not** for group-invited events. Google's `events.list` returns a DL as a single attendee entry
(the group address) plus *individually materialized* members **only as they RSVP** (`specs/0032`
Risks: "A never-RSVP'd DL may still show as one address"). `map_event` builds `attendees_json` from
`GcalAttendee`, `attendees_from_json` reshapes it to the wire `Attendee`, and `seed_from_attendees`
mints one roster row per entry.

**Landed (optimistic best-effort):**
1. **Floor (built unconditionally).** `map_event` detects a group/DL attendee — no personal display
   name + group-shaped address (a `-._+`-separated token like `team`/`all`/`dl`/`staff`), not
   `self`/resource/organizer — and writes `isDistributionList: true` into `attendees_json`. The wire
   `Attendee` carries the flag through `attendees_from_json`, and `seed_from_attendees` **excludes
   the DL from speaker/voiceprint binding** (0027 Phase 1) so no fake person is minted for the group.
   Whatever members Calendar already materialized (RSVP'd) stay as normal attendees. The manual
   "Add members" escape hatch (0027 Phase 3) remains a frontend concern on `ParticipantsPanel`.
2. **Expansion (optimistic).** When `can_expand_groups` is `true`, a post-sync pass in `sync_all`
   flattens each detected DL via **Cloud Identity** (`cloud_identity.rs`: `lookup_group` →
   `list_members`, paginated), folding the group's members into the event's attendees (deduped
   against already-materialized members and the owner emails; each group fetched once per pass). On
   `PERMISSION_DENIED`/empty it leaves the labeled floor. **Per-group best-effort:** one group's
   failure never affects another group or the sync.

**Files:** `calendar/google/sync.rs` (`attendee_is_distribution_list` + `address_looks_like_group`
detection, `expand_distribution_lists` pass), `calendar/google/cloud_identity.rs` (new client),
`calendar/google/capabilities.rs` (probe), `database/repositories/meeting_participant.rs`
(`seed_from_attendees` DL skip), `database/repositories/google_calendar.rs` (capability columns +
DL-event query/update), `frontend/src/components/Participants/ParticipantsPanel.tsx` (DL label +
"Add members" — frontend agent).

### WS3.b — Attendee photos from Google (sibling workstream)

**Current state (confirmed):** the wire `Attendee` carries name + email + `is_current_user` (+ the
new `is_distribution_list`) only; no photo field. `events.list` attendee objects do **not** include
photos; avatars in the UI (`DayAgenda.tsx AvatarStack`) are initials-only.

**Approach (best-effort, over the shared foundation):** the `directory.readonly` scope and the
`can_fetch_photos` capability flag are already in place (above). A separate agent adds the actual
**People API** fetch (`people:listDirectoryPeople` / `people:batchGet` with `photos`,`emailAddresses`)
gated on `can_fetch_photos`, caches a photo per attendee email (extend `attendees_json` — no
migration — or a small `attendee_photos(email, url, fetched_at)` cache), threads a photo URL onto the
wire shape, and renders it in `AvatarStack`. When the flag is `false`/`null`, keep initials — no
extra egress. **This foundation deliberately does not touch the People-API fetch, the wire `photo`
field, or `AvatarStack`.**

**Files:** `calendar/google/` (new People API client + cache — sibling agent),
`calendar/google/sync.rs` (thread a photo URL onto the wire shape),
`frontend/src/components/Calendar/DayAgenda.tsx` (`AvatarStack` renders photos).

## WS4 — Multi-day / week agenda (P2)

> Owner: rust-core-engineer (date-scoped agenda) + frontend-engineer (date navigation + week view).
> **Extends `specs/0036` (Today view).**

**Current state (confirmed):** `api_get_day_agenda` is hardcoded to **today**:
`today_local_bounds()` computes the range (`calendar/day_agenda.rs:297-311`) and
`MeetingsRepository::get_today_with_status(pool, start_utc, end_utc)` (`:314`) loads only today's
recordings; the Today view (`frontend/src/app/page.tsx`, 8am–6pm hour grid via
`lib/today-timeline.ts`) has no date state. Pre-call prep (0036) is generated for
today+tomorrow but the UI can only *open* today's occurrences.

**Approach:** Parameterize the agenda by date. Add `api_get_day_agenda(date: Option<String>)`
(local `YYYY-MM-DD`; `None` = today, preserving the current call sites) computing the bounds from
the passed day; add a range-scoped recordings query (or reuse `get_today_with_status` with the new
bounds — rename to `get_between_with_status`). The Today view gains **date navigation** (prev/next
day, "Today" reset, a small date picker); propose an optional **week view** (7 day-columns or a

## WS6 — Live-transcript UX (P2)

> Owner: frontend-engineer (+ audio-engineer for WS6.2's emit). **Extends `specs/0011`,

### WS6.a — Two explicit auto-scroll modes + jump-to-latest

**Current state (confirmed — largely built by 0029 WS4.1):** the single scroll owner is
`useAutoScroll` (`hooks/useAutoScroll.ts`), which already implements hysteresis — entering
"released" on user scroll (`userScrolledRef`, `nextAutoFollow` from `lib/auto-scroll.ts`, `:109-115`)
and re-following near the bottom. `VirtualizedTranscriptView` already renders a **"Jump to latest"**
pill in released mode during live recording (`VirtualizedTranscriptView.tsx:279,428-437,609-616`,
`showJumpToLatest` from `lib/auto-scroll.ts`). So the two-mode behavior and the return affordance
**exist**.

**Approach:** This is a **verify-and-refine**, not a rebuild. Confirm on a real recording that (1)
follow mode tracks new segments smoothly, (2) any user scroll-up reliably enters released mode and
the view does **not** jump on new segments, and (3) the pill returns to follow. Tune the hysteresis
thresholds / pill visibility copy if the owner still reports jumpiness; only touch
`lib/auto-scroll.ts` constants and the pill, not the architecture. If verification shows it already
satisfies the ask, this sub-item closes as "confirmed working" with the tuning notes.

**Files:** `frontend/src/hooks/useAutoScroll.ts`, `frontend/src/lib/auto-scroll.ts`,
`frontend/src/components/VirtualizedTranscriptView.tsx:428-616`.

### WS6.b — Spectrometer responsiveness + consistent bar widths

**Current state (confirmed):** the emit path was fixed in 0029 WS4.2 (raw mixed-window
`recording-spectrum`, VAD-independent). The hook `useRecordingWaveform`
(`hooks/useRecordingWaveform.ts`) resamples bands and eases bars (rise-fast/fall-slow, `:34-62`).
The render in `HeaderWaveform` (`app/record/page.tsx:67-95`) lays out bars with **`flex-1`**
(`:88`) inside a fixed-width 120px container with `gap-[2px]` over 28 bars — flex-1 distributes
fractional pixels, which browsers round **inconsistently**, so bars have visibly uneven widths.
Responsiveness: normal speech produces little movement (the ask is "boost responsiveness — little
movement for normal speech").

**Approach:** (1) **Uniform widths** — give each bar a fixed pixel width (compute
`(containerWidth − gaps) / barCount` and set an explicit `width`, or use a CSS grid with equal
tracks) instead of `flex-1`, so every bar is identical. (2) **Responsiveness** — increase the
displayed dynamic range: apply a perceptual (log/`sqrt`) mapping and a higher rise gain in
`useRecordingWaveform` so normal speech visibly moves while silence still eases to the floor;
optionally raise the emit-side gain/normalization in `audio/pipeline.rs` (audio-engineer). Apply the
same width fix to `GlobalRecordingBar.tsx`'s bars.

**Files:** `frontend/src/app/record/page.tsx:67-95` (bar width),
`frontend/src/hooks/useRecordingWaveform.ts:21-64` (perceptual mapping + gain),
`frontend/src/components/GlobalRecordingBar.tsx` (matching width fix), optionally
`frontend/src-tauri/src/audio/pipeline.rs` (emit gain/normalization).

### WS6.c — Identifying a speaker in the transcript auto-adds them to participants

**Current state (confirmed):** identifying a speaker runs through
`components/MeetingDetails/InlineSpeakerAssign.tsx` → `api_assign_speaker_to_attendee` /
`assign_speaker_to_person` (`diarization/commands.rs`), which links the speaker to a `people` row
(creating one by email if needed) but does **not** necessarily add that person to the meeting's
`meeting_participants` roster (`specs/0017`). So a speaker you name may still not appear as a meeting
participant.

**Approach:** After a successful speaker→person assignment, if that person isn't already on the
meeting roster and isn't the owner (`assignee_is_self` / owner-email exclusion, `specs/0018`), also
`MeetingParticipantsRepository::add(meeting_id, person_id, "identified")` in the same command
transaction, and refresh the participants panel. Owner-identified speakers are excluded (they're
"You", not a participant — 0018). Idempotent on re-assign.

**Files:** `frontend/src-tauri/src/diarization/commands.rs` (`assign_speaker_to_*` adds the roster
row), `frontend/src-tauri/src/database/repositories/meeting_participant.rs` (`add`, dedupe on
`(meeting_id, person_id)`), `frontend/src/components/MeetingDetails/InlineSpeakerAssign.tsx` /
`SpeakerLegend.tsx` (refresh roster after assign).
