# 0041 — 1.8 Feedback Batch: Diarization Regression, Summary Ordering, Calendar Fidelity & Transcript UX

- **Status:** Implemented 2026-07-08 (owner smoke pending) — approved same day ("work through them all")
- **Owner agent(s):** audio-engineer + rust-core-engineer + frontend-engineer + llm-pipeline-engineer (multi-workstream)
- **Roadmap phase:** 1.8 feedback batch (sixth dogfood round: 1.1←0019, 1.2←0024, 1.3←0029, 1.6←0038)

## Context / Problem

A day of real-meeting use on the **1.8.0 build** produced 11 feedback items
(2026-07-08 entries in `specs/BACKLOG.md`, which carry the full root-cause analysis and
`file:line` anchors — this spec references rather than repeats them). Three explorer passes
traced every item to specific code at spec-authoring time (tree = `main` @ `c4a896a`), so
unlike earlier batches this one starts with root causes in hand.

Two items are **regressions or races in shipped behavior** (WS1 diarization collapse,
WS2 summary/diarization ordering), one is a **shipped-but-broken feature** (WS3
record-from-detail), and one is a **never-worked feature** (WS4 prep priors). The rest are
calendar fidelity and UI-mechanics debt.

**Relationship to `specs/0039`:** 0039 WS1 (cluster consolidation) was implemented ahead of
its spec's approval (commit `61ad921`) and is the direct cause of this batch's WS1
regression. The fix here amends that implementation in place; 0039 keeps the rest of its
scope (span-level correction, voiceprint-pollution guard). This batch's WS7.2
(scroll-preservation on reassignment) fixes the *mechanics* of the existing correction UI
and does not pre-empt 0039 WS2's span-correction design.

## Goals

- Diarization no longer collapses distinct voices into the dominant speaker; the
  consolidation pass keeps its drift-repair value with a bounded, conservative merge.
- A recorded meeting ends up with a **speaker-attributed summary and resolved action-item
  owners** without user intervention, regardless of how long diarization takes.
- "Record" on a scheduled meeting's detail page behaves exactly like Join & Record from
  Home (opens the call, adopts the row), and re-recording an occurrence continues the
  existing meeting instead of minting a duplicate.
- Prep briefs actually surface prior occurrences: same-series **or** same-title matches,
  plus a manual "mark related" escape hatch, plus an explanation when nothing is found.
- One calendar event = one bubble; unwanted events are dismissible from the timeline;
  connecting a large Google account starts with a sane calendar selection.
- Back-to-back meetings render stacked, not staggered; bubbles never draw past their end
  time; the date/actions toolbar stays put while the agenda scrolls.
- "Jump to latest" locks the live transcript to follow until the *user* scrolls away;
  fixing a speaker label keeps your place.
- The recording-started notification stops wearing the Meetily icon on the owner's machine,
  with a documented remediation.

## Non-goals

- **0039's remaining scope** (span-level correction UX, create-speaker, voiceprint
  quarantine) — unchanged, stays in 0039.
- **Re-summarize after manual speaker corrections** — WS2 only re-runs after the *offline
  diarization pass*. A "Refresh summary" affordance after manual edits already exists via
  regenerate; auto-triggering it on every correction would spam LLM runs. Revisit with 0039.
- **Attendee-overlap scoring for prep matching** — WS4 ships series-key ∪ title matching +
  manual links; fuzzier heuristics wait for evidence they're needed.
- **Cross-source (EventKit↔Google) dedup** — single-active-source (ADR-0010) makes it moot;
  WS5 dedups within the active source only.
- **A general notification-icon system** — WS8 is a one-machine remediation + icon hygiene,
  not new notification features.

## Approach

Eight independent workstreams, each a surgical fix at an anchor identified in the backlog
analysis. Two touch the DB (WS2 adds one column; WS4 adds one table + one command surface);
the rest are logic/UI changes. Sequenced P1→P3 on one integration branch
(`integration/0041`), each WS its own reviewable commit(s), `/check` green per WS.

## Design

### WS1 — Diarization consolidation regression (audio-engineer)

Anchors: `diarization/sherpa.rs:222` (`CONSOLIDATE_FLOOR = 0.50`), `consolidate_clusters`
(`sherpa.rs:932-968`), call site before the `AtMost` cap (`sherpa.rs:1025-1040`).

Three changes, all in `sherpa.rs`:
1. **Raise the floor to 0.70.** 0.50 fuses same-gender distinct voices (cos 0.5–0.7 is the
   normal inter-speaker band for these embeddings); drift-split halves of the *same* voice
   sit well above 0.70. Keep it a tunable via the existing `with_consolidation_floor`.
2. **Bound the cascade.** Replace `keep_merging = |_| true` with a predicate that stops at
   the seeded expectation: in `AtMost(n)` mode never consolidate below `max(n, 2)` (the
   attendee/calendar seed is a floor, not just a cap); in `Auto` mode cap total merges per
   pass at `clusters / 2` (a drift-split repair at most halves the count).
3. **Guard the snowball:** a cluster that has already absorbed a merge this pass may not be
   the survivor of another merge unless the new pair's similarity clears `floor + 0.05` —
   breaks the duration-weighted centroid drag toward the dominant speaker.

Regression test: synthesize (or fixture) embedding sets — (a) one voice drift-split into
two clusters at cos ≥ 0.75 → must merge; (b) three distinct voices at pairwise cos ≤ 0.65
with one dominant-duration cluster → must NOT merge; (c) AtMost(3) seed with 4 clusters →
consolidation may reach 3, never 2. Extend the existing consolidation tests from `61ad921`.

**Measured (2026-07-08):** the 0.70 floor was validated on real ground truth — three local
Zoom recordings + their `.vtt` speaker-labeled transcripts (31 min / 4 speakers, 60 min /
2 speakers, 2 h 06 / 20 speakers), scored as time-weighted DER (±0.25 s collar, 10 ms
frames, greedy-by-overlap cluster→speaker mapping) by the new `eval_ground_truth_sweep`
harness in `frontend/src-tauri/tests/diarization_tuning.rs` (`#[ignore]`d; skips unless the
local `zoom-samples/` dir + models are present). Sweep of floor {0.50–0.80} × WS1 bounds
{on, off} × mode {Auto, AtMost(true n)}:

| config (bounds on)        | 4-spk DER | 2-spk DER | 20-spk DER | macro |
|---------------------------|-----------|-----------|------------|-------|
| floor 0.65–0.70, Auto     | 66.1%     | 42.4%     | 56.5%      | **55.0%** (best bounds-on Auto) |
| floor 0.75–0.80, Auto     | 66.1–66.5%| 52.8%     | 56.9–58.4% | 58.6–59.2% |
| floor ≤ 0.60, Auto        | 66.7–66.9%| 42.4%     | 56.5%      | 55.2% |
| any floor, AtMost(true n) | 43.6%     | 41.1%     | 56.5%      | **47.1%** |

Conclusions: **0.70 stays** (0.65/0.70 tie for the optimum; 0.75+ regresses the 2-speaker
file 42%→53% because genuine drift-splits stop merging). WS1 bounds cost ≤ 0.6 pp macro DER
vs unbounded (kept — they exist to prevent the collapse regression, which these three files
happen not to trigger). The floor is a second-order knob on real data: the AtMost(n)
calendar seed dominates (47.1% vs 55.0% macro), and absolute DER is dominated by *raw*
clustering impurity upstream of consolidation — at the shipped Auto threshold 0.8 the
dominant raw cluster on the 4-speaker file is only ~55% pure (mixes 3 voices), which no
post-hoc floor can repair. Per-file DER at the shipped defaults is pinned (+~3 pp headroom)
in `eval_regression_gate` in the same file. Follow-up candidates (out of WS1 scope):
re-tune `DEFAULT_AUTO_THRESHOLD` on this ground truth (`VINYL_EVAL_RAW_THRESHOLD` knob is
in the harness), and revisit `MERGE_FLOOR = 0.30` — measured distinct-voice centroid pairs
reach cos 0.62, so the AtMost cap path can fuse real speakers (the 4-speaker file caps to
4 clusters but 98% of speech lands in one).

### WS2 — Summary waits for / refreshes with speakers (rust-core + llm-pipeline + frontend)

Anchors: `useRecordingStop.ts:469-480`, `meeting-details/page.tsx:148+`,
`lib/auto-summary.ts:44-54`, `summary/service.rs:427-456`, `diarization/pipeline.rs:885`
(`diarization-complete`).

**Decision: hybrid (option c).** Summarize immediately (fast feedback survives), then
regenerate once with speakers when diarization lands:
- **Data:** add `speaker_attributed INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0` to the summary-process
  table (new forward migration). Set at generation time from the existing `any_speaker`
  computation in `service.rs`.
- **Trigger (backend-owned, survives page navigation):** at the successful end of the
  offline diarization pipeline (where `diarization-complete` is emitted), if the meeting's
  latest completed summary has `speaker_attributed = 0` AND the pass actually assigned
  speakers, re-run the summary through the same path auto-summary uses (same template, same
  provider config), emitting the existing progress events. Action items re-extract through
  the 0034 regeneration-safe diff (user-completed/edited items untouched).
- **Pristine guard:** skip the auto-regenerate if the summary text was user-edited since
  generation (reuse whatever edited-flag/confirm-before-regenerate signal 0020 relies on;
  if none exists cheaply, compare stored generation hash — implementer's call, documented
  in code).
- **UI:** while the re-run is in flight, the summary panel shows a quiet "Updating with
  speaker names…" state (reuse the existing generation-progress plumbing — no new event
  surface). No blocking, no modal.
- Re-entrancy: the regenerated summary also computes `speaker_attributed`; since it now has
  speakers it sets 1 and the trigger can't loop.

### WS3 — Record-from-detail parity + no duplicates (frontend + rust-core)

Anchors: `ScheduledRecordControl.tsx:79-87`, `lib/calendar.ts:372-445`,
`repositories/meeting.rs:110-130, 238-257`, `api/api.rs:1221-1296`,
`page-content.tsx:384-391`.

1. **Launch the call:** thread the event's meeting URL into the control. The detail page
   knows `calendarEventId`; resolve the event's `zoomUrl`/meeting link the same way the
   Home agenda item gets it (lookup by event key through the day-agenda/upcoming source)
   and pass it in the `joinAndRecord` event object. If the event has no link, behave as
   today (record only) — the control's "link-agnostic" copy becomes true instead of
   aspirational.
2. **Retry = continue, not create:** in `ScheduledRecordControl` (and the detail page's
   state), if the occurrence's meeting row is already `origin='recorded'` **with
   transcripts**, the button becomes **Continue recording** and routes through the 0037
   resume/continue path (same `meeting_id`, new segment) instead of `joinAndRecord`'s
   create. The false-start case ("started recording by mistake, stopped, want to redo")
   is thereby *additive* — no duplicate row, the summary re-runs over combined segments
   (0037 semantics).
3. **Timestamp hygiene:** pass the occurrence start as RFC3339 (or omit it) so
   `api_create_meeting`'s day-window match doesn't silently shift to `Utc::now()`
   (`api.rs:1206-1219`).

### WS4 — Prep priors: additive matching + manual association (rust-core + frontend)

Anchors: `repositories/meeting.rs:158-202` (`find_prior_series_occurrences`),
`aggregation/prep_jobs.rs:108-167`, `prep_commands.rs:232-244`.

1. **Additive matcher:** replace the `if series_key / else if title` shadowing with a
   **union**: `(calendar_series_key = ?1 OR normalized_title = ?2 OR manually linked)`,
   still `origin='recorded'` + HAS_CONTENT + `created_at < before`, newest-first, deduped
   by meeting id, same LIMIT. Title normalization stays `LOWER(TRIM(...))`.
2. **Manual association:** new table `meeting_series_links (meeting_id TEXT PRIMARY KEY
   REFERENCES meetings(id) ON DELETE CASCADE, series_key TEXT NOT NULL)` — a meeting can be
   pinned into a series regardless of title/calendar. New commands
   `api_link_meeting_to_series(meeting_id, target_meeting_id_or_series_key)` /
   `api_unlink_meeting_from_series(meeting_id)` (registered in `lib.rs`); linking picks the
   target's `calendar_series_key` if present else a synthetic `manual:{uuid}` key stamped
   on both. The matcher's union includes this table.
3. **UI:** on the meeting page's Prep tab (and the detail header's overflow menu), a
   "Link previous meeting…" action opening a small search/pick list (recent meetings,
   FTS-backed title filter). When a prep brief comes back empty, the panel says *why*
   ("No previous meetings found for this series — link one") with the same action, instead
   of rendering nothing (`status='none'` path).
4. **Regenerate on link:** creating a link invalidates the cached brief for the upcoming
   occurrence (the existing `source_fingerprint` machinery should pick this up — verify).

### WS5 — Calendar dedup, timeline dismiss, selection defaults (rust-core + frontend)

Anchors: `repositories/google_calendar.rs:206-222`, `calendar/day_agenda.rs:164-245,
398-431`, `eventkit.rs:299-461`, `calendar/google/sync.rs:576-626`,
`migrations/20260704000000_add_google_calendar.sql:25`, `CalendarSettings.tsx:492-510`,
`app/page.tsx` (`TimelineBlock`).

1. **Dedup by iCalUID at the read path:** in `cached_upcoming_between` (Google) and the
   EventKit read, collapse events sharing `ical_uid`/`external_id` + start time to one,
   preferring the row whose calendar is the event's organizer/primary (else first). The
   dismiss key already normalizes to `ext:{iCalUID}@{starts_at}`, so dismissals keep
   working across the collapse.
2. **Dismiss from the timeline:** add the 0026 hide affordance to `TimelineBlock` (context
   menu or hover ⋯) for unrecorded calendar items (`canHide` semantics from the dead
   `DayAgenda.tsx:306-308`), calling the existing `dismissCalendarEvent`; an undo toast
   mirrors the old component. Delete the now-fully-dead `Calendar/DayAgenda.tsx` if nothing
   else imports it (fork-cleanup hygiene, verify first).
3. **Selection defaults:** in `api_google_calendar_connect`'s list sync: if the account
   exposes **more than 5 calendars**, register all with `selected = 0` except the primary
   calendar (`primary = true` from the calendarList API), which stays selected; ≤5 keeps
   today's all-selected behavior. Implementation: `upsert_sync_state` gains an explicit
   `selected` default for *new* rows (existing rows keep their user-set value). Settings
   gains **Select all / none** bulk actions above the checkbox list. Existing installs are
   untouched (no migration of current selections).

### WS6 — Timeline geometry + sticky toolbar (frontend)

Anchors: `lib/today-timeline.ts:21-23, 97-117, 266-314`, `app/page.tsx:680-921`,
`lib/__tests__/today-timeline.test.ts`.

1. **Lanes from true times:** `assignLanes` clusters/overlaps on **time intervals**
   (`[start, end)`, end-exclusive so 11:00/11:00 never collides), not rendered pixels.
2. **No overshoot:** bubble height stays floored at a legible minimum, but the floor
   shrinks (`MIN_ITEM_PX` 34 → 28) and short adjacent meetings resolve *visual* crowding by
   a push-down within the gap (next bubble's render top may shift down up to the
   min-height delta) rather than lanes — matching the owner's "stack, don't stagger; add
   spacing" ask. Rendered bottom must never imply a longer duration than `MIN_ITEM_PX`
   requires; update the pinned expectations in `today-timeline.test.ts`.
3. **Sticky toolbar:** lift the date-nav/quick-actions row (`page.tsx:726-818`) out of the
   `overflow-y-auto` region as a `flex-shrink-0` sibling (same treatment as the greeting
   header), preserving the `max-w-[780px]` alignment.

### WS7 — Transcript follow-lock + scroll preservation (frontend)

Anchors: `hooks/useAutoScroll.ts`, `lib/auto-scroll.ts`,
`VirtualizedTranscriptView.tsx:279-520`, `MeetingDetails/TranscriptPanel.tsx:82-131`.

1. **Sticky follow intent:** `useAutoScroll` gains an explicit `followLocked` flag. Jump-
   to-latest sets it; while set, new segments always scroll to bottom (no proximity check).
   It clears **only on user-initiated upward scroll** — discriminate via `wheel`/`touchmove`
   /keyboard listeners (not `scroll` events, which fire for programmatic and content-growth
   scrolls). The 100px vs 8/24px threshold split collapses into the one hysteresis helper.
   Natural-follow (user is simply at the bottom) keeps the existing hysteresis behavior.
2. **Scroll-preserving corrections:** correction handlers stop refetching the world. On a
   successful `api_set_segment_speaker(s)`/assign, patch the affected segment objects in
   place (the server result is deterministic) and keep `onRefetchTranscripts` only as a
   background reconciliation that must **preserve the pagination window and scroll top**
   (no page-one reset). Verify no container key remounts on speaker-identity change.

### WS8 — Notification icon remediation (rust-core + docs)

Anchors: `notifications/system.rs:34-41`, `tauri.conf.json:5, 87-91`, ADR-0004.

1. **Remediation script** `scripts/reset-notification-icon-cache.sh`: `lsregister -f` the
   installed production app, clear the NotificationCenter attribution cache
   (`killall NotificationCenter usernoted` after re-registration), print verification
   steps. Owner runs it once against the production install; document in ADR-0004's

2. **Icon hygiene:** attempt an explicit `.icon()` on the notification builder if

   plugin can't do it cleanly, skip — the bundle icon is authoritative once the cache is
   reset, and we don't hand-roll notification internals for a cosmetic).

## Risks / open questions

- **WS1 floor value:** 0.70 is an informed default, not a measured one; the regression
  fixtures pin behavior, but a real-meeting validation pass (owner smoke on a known
  multi-speaker recording) is the true gate. Tunable is kept precisely for this.
- **WS2 pristine detection:** if no cheap user-edited signal exists, the generation-hash
  compare adds a small surface; acceptable.
- **WS3 continue-path coupling:** reuses 0037's resume machinery on a *cleanly stopped*
  meeting — that's its designed "continue recording" case, but the detail-page entry is a
  new caller; watch the recording-state assumptions.
- **WS5 organizer preference:** picking which duplicate row survives is a heuristic;
  wrong pick only affects which calendar's copy supplies metadata, not whether it shows.
- **WS6 push-down layout:** shifting a render top down by up to the min-height delta
  slightly desyncs the bubble from the hour grid for tightly packed short meetings —
  accepted trade (owner explicitly preferred spacing over stagger).
