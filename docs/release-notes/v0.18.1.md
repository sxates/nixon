### Topics

**Topics** follow a project, client or subject across all your Meetings, with one place for its notes, decisions and To-Dos. Link a Topic to Meetings or a whole Series, and after each one Nixon adds a dated entry with the decisions called out. When a Topic comes up in a Meeting you didn't link, Nixon suggests a note for it, with a link to the moment it was said. Star the Topics you care about to keep them in the sidebar, in the order you choose, and jump to any Topic from ⌘K.

### Your To-Dos, organized your way

Nixon suggests action items from your Meetings and merges the ones that come up more than once, so you add each task to your list once. Arrange your To-Do list by dragging, group it by Topic, and move a To-Do to another Topic by dropping it there. Add due dates, @mention People, highlight what matters, and keep a Topic's To-Dos on its own page when they don't belong on your main list.
